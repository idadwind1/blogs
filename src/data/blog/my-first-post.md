## Brief Intro

This is actually not the first time that I've blogged. Way back in August 2022, I already created a website using Jekyll, but it didn't go well and was soon forgotten. This year, 2026, I decided to start writing blogs again thanks to my friend [zhdbk3](https://zhdbk3.github.io). She gave me inspiration for the website.

Although the old one is deprecated, you can still access its repo at [GitHub](https://www.github.com/idadwind1/idadwind1.github.io). It wasn't well made because I wasn't so good at programming at that time.


Anyway, for this blog I will be writing about how I am going to organize my website, what I will be writing about, and little facts about me.

## Organizing The Website
As mentioned in the website's index page, I am multilingual (specifically, I speak three languages: Chinese, English, and Japanese), which means posts might be in different languages. I will be mainly writing in English and sometimes Chinese. Japanese will be the least used language I will be writing in. Each post will be attached with *tags* of either `en`, `cn`, or `ja` so that you can filter a specific language in the `/tags` page.

> fun fact: I was originally going to add a mermaid chart renderer to the website because I did so in my old website, but I thought it was not necessary so I ended up not adding it.

## What to Write
I do not have a fixed topic to write about, but I will say this website is going to have a lot of posts about coding and programming, math, and AI. Besides those, I might also be writing about my life, my thoughts, and opinions (**not political**—I hate political stuff).

## About Me
I was born in China, but at a very young age (I believe it was 5 or 6), I started speaking English, so English is like my second mother tongue. I started learning Japanese, on the other hand, only recently.
I am terrible at writing (regardless of which language). I am bad at structuring my paragraphs. You might expect to see me struggling to explain something through text :( but that's fine, I believe I can write just as well as [she](https://zhdbk3.github.io) if I keep writing :3
