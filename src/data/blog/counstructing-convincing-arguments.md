In everyday life, people argue about almost everything—politics, opinions, and even scientific facts. Surprisingly, some arguments remain highly convincing even when they are not logically rigorous or technically sound. Why does this happen? How can arguments that lack strong reasoning still persuade people? In this blog, I will explain how these type of argument is made to be convincing, and how we can too construct this type of argument.

## Rhetoric
It is important to note that when it comes to how to convince people, we are talking about **rhetoric** rather than **logic**. Rhetoric, defined by the *Oxford English Dictionary*, is:

> the art of using language effectively and persuasively, particularly in speaking or writing to influence others.

Logic, on the other hand, focuses more on rigor, accuracy, and precision of diction and language.
